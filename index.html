First & Co-Corresponding Author Cardiovascular AI

A Diagnosis-Independent Severity Phenotype in Cardiovascular Admissions Using Unsupervised Machine Learning and Survival Analysis

Authors & Affiliations:
Faria Javed1*, Fatime Taj2, Adnan Yousaf3, Sara Aslam1*
1 Dept. of Biomedical Engineering, Superior University Lahore • * Corresponding Author

Identifies distinct, diagnosis-independent physiological severity clusters among acute cardiovascular admissions using unsupervised machine learning. By coupling phenotypic clustering with Kaplan-Meier and Cox proportional hazards survival analysis, the study demonstrates superior dynamic risk stratification for clinical triage compared to standard ICD admission classifications.

Unsupervised Phenotyping Clusters multi-parameter hemodynamics and vital biomarkers independent of admission labels.
Survival Analysis Evaluates endpoints via Kaplan-Meier curves and Cox proportional hazards models.
Unsupervised ML Survival Analysis (Cox PH) Cardiovascular Admissions Severity Phenotyping Clinical Informatics
Peer-Reviewed
Pharmacogenomics & Oncology Pediatric Oncology

Polygenic Risk Score Matches Machine-Learning Discrimination Without Overfitting in Predicting Chemotherapy-Induced Hepatotoxicity in Pediatrics Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia

Authors & Affiliations:
Sara Aslam1*, Sumbal Sarwar2, Adnan Yousaf3, Faria Javed1, Shabana4*
1 Dept. of Biomedical Engineering, Superior University Lahore • * Corresponding Author

Investigates predictive discrimination of Polygenic Risk Scores (PRS) versus supervised machine learning algorithms in anticipating chemotherapy-induced hepatotoxicity in pediatric Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL). Demonstrates that linear additive genomic risk scoring matches complex non-linear ML discrimination while actively avoiding overfitting.

PRS vs. ML Benchmarking Comparative AUROC and sensitivity profiles across pediatric cohort genomes.
Overfitting Mitigation Guarantees generalizable clinical translation without high-dimensional feature leakage.
Polygenic Risk Score (PRS) Pediatric ALL Chemotherapy Hepatotoxicity Machine Learning Discrimination Precision Oncology
